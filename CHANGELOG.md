### Added

* Initial native Gauge reporter gRPC implementation.
* Hybrid live/final reconciliation with parallel and retry correlation.
* Official Allure result/container model, stable identities, nested concepts, hooks, tables, metadata, messages, and screenshots.
* Guarded output cleaning, atomic writes, attachment containment/redaction, best-effort and strict modes.
* Gauge Python exhaustive and Gauge JavaScript smoke fixtures.
* Cross-platform packaging, compatibility policy, CI/release/security automation, documentation, and governance.
