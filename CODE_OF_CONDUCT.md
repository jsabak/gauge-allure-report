# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make our community welcoming, safe, and equitable for all. We respect the dignity, rights, and contributions of every individual regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socioeconomic position, education, or other status. Everyone participating in good faith under this Covenant receives the same privileges of participation.

## Encouraged behavior

We acknowledge that culture, background, and native language affect how words and actions are interpreted. Participants should:

1. respect the community’s purpose and ways of gathering;
2. engage kindly and honestly;
3. respect different viewpoints and experiences;
4. take responsibility for actions and contributions;
5. give and accept constructive feedback gracefully;
6. repair harm when it occurs; and
7. promote the community’s well-being.

## Restricted behavior

Instances, threats, or promotion of the following violate this Code:

1. harassment, including violating expressed boundaries or continuing unwanted personal attention;
2. character attacks or insulting, demeaning, or pejorative comments;
3. stereotyping or discrimination based on identities or traits;
4. inappropriate sexualization;
5. disclosure or misuse of private information;
6. causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm;
7. impersonation or identity deception used to evade enforcement;
8. failing to credit contributed sources;
9. out-of-context commercial promotion;
10. irresponsible presentation of content involving restricted behavior; or
11. other conduct that threatens community well-being.

## Reporting

Not every disagreement is a Code of Conduct violation. When a possible violation occurs, report it promptly and privately to the maintainers at `jan.sabak@amberteam.pl`. Do not create a public issue containing private or harmful material.

Moderators will take reports seriously, investigate available messages, logs, recordings, and witness accounts, and prioritize safety and confidentiality. Enforcement is handled privately with the involved parties; broader communication may form part of an agreed repair.

## Addressing and repairing harm

Moderators may use this ladder according to impact and severity, skipping levels when necessary:

1. **Warning** — a private written warning for an incident, with repair such as acknowledgement, apology, or clarification of expectations.
2. **Temporarily limited activities** — a time-limited cooldown or restricted interactions after repetition or a more serious first incident, with reflection and careful re-entry.
3. **Temporary suspension** — removal from community spaces with explicit return conditions after repeated or serious violations.
4. **Permanent ban** — removal from all community spaces when prior remedies cannot preserve safety or a violation is exceptionally severe.

This ladder is guidance and does not remove moderator discretion in the community’s best interests.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces and when a person officially represents the project, including through an official account, address, event, or public role.
